# Evidence Boundary

- simulation baseline: complete
- hardware-derived model: complete
- IBM runtime backend: complete
- cross-repo interpretation: present but separate

Experiment 6 now has three completed evidence lanes: a local simulation
baseline, a calibration-anchored hardware-derived model, and a real IBM runtime
backend phase. Those lanes support the bounded claim that coherent structured
patterns preserve more of their form under disturbance than randomized controls
do.

What they do not do by themselves is settle the full ontology, prove a single
mechanism for consciousness, or collapse the broader Codex 67 architecture
layer into the pulse question. The wider spiritual-attractor-overlap diagnosis
and the broader paper-level architecture remain interpretive layers that sit
beside these measured or modeled outputs rather than replacing them.
